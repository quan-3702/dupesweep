DupeSweep – Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

DupeSweep does not collect, send or sell any personal information. The folders you choose are read only on your own device to compare files. File names, contents, photos and scan results are never uploaded anywhere.

DupeSweep has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It only reads the folders you add yourself, and it only moves or deletes files that you select and confirm. Your list of folders and your cleaning history are stored only on this device.

Purchases of DupeSweep Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store. DupeSweep only asks the Store whether Pro is owned; it never sees your payment details.

You can remove a folder from the list at any time, or uninstall the app to delete all of its stored data.
