🧹 How to use DupeSweep

DupeSweep finds files that are stored more than once on your PC – photos copied from your phone twice, downloads saved again, backups of backups – so you can free up space safely. Everything happens on this computer.

1. Add folders

Click ＋ Add a folder or drive and pick a folder such as Pictures, Downloads or Documents, or a whole drive like D:. Windows asks you to allow DupeSweep to view and edit that folder – DupeSweep needs this to move or delete the copies you choose. You can add several folders; duplicates between them are found too. The list is remembered for next time.

Windows does not allow apps to open the system folders C:\Windows or C:\Program Files. That is a good thing – never remove files there.

2. Choose what to look for

Look for all files or only photos, videos, music, documents or archives. Small files under 100 KB are skipped by default – change this under Ignore files smaller than. Hidden folders and folders like node_modules are skipped unless you change the settings.

3. Scan

Click Scan for duplicates. DupeSweep first groups files of the same size, then compares their contents byte by byte (SHA-256), so files with different names are found and files that only look alike are never mixed up. Big scans can take a while; you can stop at any time.

4. Review

Each group shows every copy with its folder, date and size. DupeSweep marks the oldest copy in each group as keep and the others as remove. Click a thumbnail to preview a file. Change the ticks yourself, or use Auto-select to keep the newest, oldest, shortest path or the copy in a folder you choose (Pro). If every copy of a file is selected, DupeSweep warns you before anything happens.

5. Move or delete

Move selected to review folder is the safe choice: the files go into a folder called “DupeSweep review” plus the date, inside the scanned folder. Look through it, then delete it yourself. Delete selected removes the files permanently – they do not go to the Recycle Bin. ☰ More → Cleaning history lists everything DupeSweep moved or deleted.

Similar photos (Pro)

The Similar photos tab finds pictures that look the same but are not identical files – resized copies, edited versions, screenshots of the same photo and burst shots. Nothing is selected here automatically; pick the ones you don't need, or use Keep the largest.

Large files and empty folders

Large files lists the 200 biggest files in the scanned folders – handy for finding old videos and installers. Empty folders lists folders with no files in them; removing them is part of Pro.
